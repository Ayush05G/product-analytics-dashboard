## 1. Visitor-level funnel

Counts distinct *people* who reached each stage, not raw events.

| Stage | Visitors | Step conversion | Cumulative |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Viewed a product | 1,404,179 | — | 100% |
| Added to cart | 37,722 | 2.69% | 2.69% |
| Purchased | 11,719 | 28.04% | 0.83% |

**Cart→purchase caveat (matters — this is the headline metric).** 1,143 of 11,719 buyers (9.8%) have no recorded add-to-cart event at all. Dividing *all* buyers by cart-adders counts them in the numerator while they were never in the denominator, giving an inflated **31.07%**. The step conversion quoted above is progression-based — buyers who *also* carted (10,576) over cart-adders = **28.04%** — and is the figure the case study uses. (The dashboard's `metrics.funnel()` reports the naive variant; it is not wrong for a stage-count view, just a different question.)

- Visitors with no recorded `view` event: 3,401 (0.24%) — why `view→purchase` (viewer-denominated) and `kpis.conversion_rate` (all-visitor-denominated) differ slightly.
- Transaction event rows over the period: **22,457**
- Distinct orders (`transactionid`): **17,672**
- Orders per month: **3,921**
- Distinct purchasers per month: **2,600**

## 2. Segment cuts of cart→purchase

### 2a. New vs returning (visitor grain)

`returning` = had activity on a calendar day strictly *before* their first
add-to-cart day; `new` = first-ever event on that same day. Judged at the
moment of intent to avoid lookahead bias (purchasing itself generates later
activity, which would inflate a whole-window 'returning' definition).

| Segment | Cart visitors | Purchasers | Cart→purchase |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| new | 32,106 | 8,476 | 26.40% |
| returning | 5,616 | 2,100 | 37.39% |

### 2b. Single-item vs multi-item carts (visitor-day grain)

No session/cart id exists in the data, so a cart is approximated by the
visitor-day. `multi-item` = ≥2 distinct itemids added that day. Primary
conversion is same-day; `conv (7d)` allows the purchase within 7 days as a
robustness check.

| Segment | Cart-days | Purchases | Cart→purchase (same-day) | conv (7d) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| single-item | 33,991 | 8,756 | 25.76% | 27.28% |
| multi-item | 7,638 | 2,821 | 36.93% | 40.64% |

### 2c. Crossed: status × cart size (visitor-day grain, same-day)

| Status | Cart size | Cart-days | Purchases | Cart→purchase |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| new | single-item | 27,266 | 6,507 | 23.86% |
| new | multi-item | 4,840 | 1,436 | 29.67% |
| returning | single-item | 6,725 | 2,249 | 33.44% |
| returning | multi-item | 2,798 | 1,385 | 49.50% |

### Worst-converting segments

- By status: **new** at 26.40%
- By cart size: **single-item** at 25.76%
- Crossed: **new / single-item** at 23.86% (27,266 cart-days)

## 4. Cross-checks

- `metrics.kpis()['conversion_rate']` = 0.83% = buyers / all visitors; funnel view→purchase = 0.83% = buyers / viewers. The gap is exactly the no-view visitors noted above — two independent paths, reconciled.
- Funnel stages are monotonic: 1,404,179 ≥ 37,722 ≥ 11,719
- All stages ≤ total visitors (1,407,580)
